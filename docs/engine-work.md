## E5 Deferred projectile explosions lose projectile.hit events

Keep the mutation lease and bounded deferral; do not bypass persistence safety or have the game reconstruct hits by matching explosion coordinates. Cover immediate/deferred, direct/splash, nonlethal/lethal, and owner-unloaded cases.
